# Prospect register — qualified funding prospects

**Status:** Phase 2 working register, desk research only, compiled 2026-10-15. No outreach has been made to any prospect. Nothing in this register is committed; the tiers are the researcher's honest reading of published eligibility and giving history, not expressions of interest from any funder.

**How to read the tiers**

- **Committed** — a signed pledge, grant agreement or sponsorship contract exists. *None at this date.*
- **Plausible** — the school entity (a new registered charity operating an independent school for students with intellectual and developmental learning needs) fits the published eligibility, the funder gives in Hastings–Prince Edward or province-wide at a relevant scale, and there is a credible route to ask. A plausible prospect still has a low single-digit to ~30 % probability per ask.
- **Speculative** — eligibility is unconfirmed, the funder is invitation-only, the fit is partial (e.g., only a side programme such as a garden or sport qualifies), or the award size is immaterial to a $2M+ operating budget.

**Standing constraints that shape every row** (all VERIFIED in v1 unless noted; see `reports/2026-10-14-v1-research-notes/funding_and_financing.md`, hereafter "v1 F&F", and `gap_funding_verification.md`, "v1 GAP")

1. No Ontario or federal programme funds the operating costs of an independent school. Everything available to the entity is project, capital or wage-subsidy money (v1 F&F KQ1).
2. Most institutional funders require 12 months of incorporated/charitable history and one or two years of financial statements; the first realistic institutional asks are therefore 2028–29 (v1 F&F KQ1).
3. Family-directed programmes (SSAH, OAP, Passport, ODSP, CDB, ACSD) are not school revenue and cannot pay tuition; they are excluded from this register (v1 F&F KQ2, FOUNDER DECISION: no tuition).
4. CRA split-receipting: sponsorship with promotional value is not receiptable; keep donation and sponsorship menus separate (v1 F&F KQ4).
5. The founder's local business relationships are a real asset but are recorded here only as a route; no individual is named and no private information is recorded (brief, Rules of engagement).

**Key new verifications this phase**

- **OTF excludes schools.** The OTF Eligibility Policy lists among ineligible organizations "universities, colleges, schools and hospitals, and their affiliated agencies" and "organizations that have a primary education mandate," defined as "school or home-based curriculum-based education (privately or publicly delivered)"; it also excludes "organizations with less than one year (twelve months) of incorporation, operating and financial history." — VERIFIED, [OTF Eligibility Policy](https://otf.ca/node/107), accessed 2026-10-15. This moves OTF from "plausible after one year" (v1) to **Speculative**, viable only if a separately incorporated non-school affiliate ran community programmes — and even then the "affiliated agencies" exclusion applies. **CONFIRM** with OTF programme staff before any structuring.
- **United Way HPE** Community Impact Fund annual investment process "OPENS OCT 23, 2026" (close date not published). — VERIFIED, [UWHPE investment opportunities](https://unitedwayhpe.ca/investment-opportunities/), accessed 2026-10-15.
- **Enabling Accessibility Fund** 2026 call closed 2026-03-16; programme page (modified 2026-01-16) announces no next intake. — VERIFIED, [EAF CFP](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/funding/enabling-accessibility-fund-contribution.html) and [EAF programme page](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/enabling-accessibility-fund.html), accessed 2026-10-15.
- **A $109M private foundation sits in Belleville.** The John M. & Bernice Parrott Foundation reported total assets of $109,304,763 and gifts to qualified donees of $5,598,680 for FPE 2023-12-31 (CRA T3010 open data, VERIFIED, accessed 2026-10-15) and describes its purpose as benefiting "quality of life of people in the Quinte area" through health care, cultural, recreational and individual organization projects; it gave $750,000 to the Picton hospital campaign in 2021 (SECONDARY, [Picton Gazette, 2021-06-30](https://pictongazette.ca/post/parrott-foundation-bolsters-back-the-build-with-750000-donation)). It is the single most important prospect in the region and is not in the v1 notes.
- A second Belleville private foundation, the Maurice & Marilyn Rollins Foundation, received $1,250,000 in receipted gifts and granted $347,000 in FY2023 (T3010, VERIFIED). A Prince Edward County community foundation (The County Foundation, BN 843496290RR0001) exists and granted $974,272 in FY2023 (T3010, VERIFIED) — PEC-only scope.
- The Kiwanis Club of Belleville **dissolved** in October 2025 after 100+ years, distributing its final funds ($45,407.69 to BGH, "over $55,000" to The Children's Foundation). — VERIFIED, [InQuinte, 2025-10-27](https://www.inquinte.ca/?p=2274). Remove from prospect lists.

**Honest reading (PROPOSAL):** the register confirms the v1 conclusion that institutional money (government, United Way, national foundations) cannot fund Years 1–2 and is programme- or capital-sized thereafter. What is new is that the region does contain capacity at the "transformational" level — one private foundation granting ≈ $5.6M a year in the Quinte area, a second granting ≈ $0.35M, and documented individual bequests of $1.45M — so the plan's dependence on a small number of very large gifts is not fanciful, but it rests on perhaps three to five relationships and should be treated as a gate, not a forecast.
