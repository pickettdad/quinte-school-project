# Running list — questions for a future conversation with MPP Allsopp

Working list only; not a meeting brief. Political relationships are not funding commitments. Items are added as research surfaces them; each notes the ministry or agency that actually administers the matter.

## Transportation (from research/transportation.md, 2026-10-15)
- **[MTO]** Must a private school's own bus used daily for home-to-school transport be a chrome-yellow "school bus" (HTA s.175 / Reg. 612: SCHOOL BUS signs, stop arm), or may a white accessible minibus be operated as a "school-purposes bus"? Same for a van carrying six or more children.
- **[MTO]** What exterior lettering or sponsor recognition does Reg. 612 permit on a school bus and on a school-purposes vehicle?
- **[MTO]** Periodic inspection interval for a privately owned school-purposes bus, and whether O. Reg. 629 (Accessible Vehicles) applies.
- **[Ministry of Education / Tri-Board]** Can a consortium or board sell seats at cost on existing special-needs routes to private-school students, and under what authority?
- **[Ministry of Education]** Does any provincial transportation funding follow a student with a disability to a private school? (Expected no — confirm.)
- **[Quinte West / Belleville]** Can Quinte Transit's specialised service and Belleville Transit's mobility service be booked for daily school trips by eligible private-school students, and at what fare?
- **[WSIB]** Classification of an in-house school-bus driver employed by a registered-charity school ($0.33 Educational Services vs $1.43 passenger transport).
